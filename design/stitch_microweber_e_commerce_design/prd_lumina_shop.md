# Product Requirements Document (PRD): Lumina Shop

## 1. Project Overview
**Project Name:** Lumina Shop  
**Platform:** Desktop Web (Optimized for Microweber CMS)  
**Industry:** E-commerce / Premium Lifestyle Goods  
**Target Audience:** Consumers seeking high-quality, minimalist lifestyle products (electronics, watches, accessories).

## 2. Product Vision
Lumina Shop bertujuan untuk memberikan pengalaman berbelanja yang mewah melalui kesederhanaan. Fokus utama adalah pada kejernihan visual, navigasi tanpa hambatan, dan alur konversi yang efisien, memanfaatkan kemampuan drag-and-drop dan manajemen konten dari Microweber CMS.

## 3. Design System & Brand Identity
- **Name:** Lumina Commerce
- **Primary Color:** Navy (#1E3A8A)
- **Typography:** Inter (Sans-serif)
- **Visual Style:** Minimalist, high-contrast, rounded corners (8px), generous whitespace.

## 4. Information Architecture (Screen List)
### 4.1 Homepage
- **Hero Section:** High-impact banner with CTA.
- **Featured Collections:** Visual categories (Active Performance, Audio Tech, Home Office).
- **New Arrivals:** Grid of latest products with quick-add functionality.

### 4.2 Product Catalog
- **Filtering System:** Sidebar with Category, Price Range, Size, and Color filters.
- **Product Grid:** Clear pricing, titles, and rating summaries.
- **Pagination:** "Load More" functionality for performance.

### 4.3 Product Detail Page
- **Media Gallery:** Multi-image viewer with "New Arrival" badges.
- **Product Info:** Title, SKU, price, reviews, and detailed description.
- **Variants:** Color swatches and quantity selector.
- **Tabs:** Specifications, Reviews, and Shipping & Returns.
- **Cross-selling:** "Related Products" section.

### 4.4 Checkout
- **Order Summary:** Real-time calculation of subtotal, shipping, and tax.
- **Multi-step Form (Single Page):**
    - Step 1: Shipping Information.
    - Step 2: Payment Method (Credit Card/PayPal).
- **Security:** SSL payment badges for trust.

## 5. Functional Requirements
- **CMS Integration:** Elements must be compatible with Microweber's module system.
- **Search:** Global search in the top navigation.
- **Cart Management:** Persistent shopping cart with item count indicator.
- **Responsiveness:** While primarily desktop-focused, the layout must be adaptable to mobile and tablet.

## 6. Success Metrics
- **Conversion Rate:** High focus on the streamlined checkout process.
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** Encouraged through related products and featured collections.
- **Page Load Speed:** Optimized through clean HTML/CSS and efficient asset management.
